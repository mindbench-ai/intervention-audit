  • Antidepressants (e.g., SSRIs): These are not indicated for uncomplicated grief. Grief is not major depression. Common first-line options

Start at a low dose because initial activation can temporarily worsen anxiety:

  • Escitalopram: 5 mg daily, increasing to 10 mg; usual range 10–20 mg/day
